Bid brief · Form 470 #270003851 · posted November 6, 2026

Lake Zurich Community Unit School District 95

Lake Zurich, Illinois. New Cisco Catalyst 9200L switches, or equivalent, plus stacking kits for seven schools. Hardware is the base bid; installation is priced separately as an option.

  • Dec 8proposals due, 12:00 noon CST
  • 56switches
  • 7schools
  • 40%district E-Rate discount

At a glance

They want
Layer 3 switches that can handle BGP, to replace older core switches, plus stacking kits with 1 m and 3 m cables
Base bid
Hardware, licenses and warranty only. The district plans to do design, installation and configuration itself.
Install options
Price installation three ways: (1) high school and both middle schools, (2) the four elementary schools, (3) all seven schools
Timeline
Installation after the E-Rate funding decision and after April 1, 2027
Send it
By email to the four people named in the RFP (two district staff, two at the district's E-Rate consultant). Put the Form 470 number and the district name in the subject line.
Questions
Technical: the district's Assistant Director of Technology. E-Rate: Telesolutions Consultants. Contacts are in the RFP.
Invoicing
District picks SPI or BEAR at signing; your contract must include a line for each

Check these before you price

  1. Stacking kit counts do not match. The scope summary asks for 22 kits with 3 m cable and 24 with 1 m. The school-by-school list adds up to 24 and 28. Ask the consultant in writing which is right, or price the per-school list.
  2. Typo at May Whitney Elementary. The RFP says "Two (4)" switches. The 54-switch total only works with 4.
  3. Installation is both in and out. The RFP says the district does the labor, but also asks for installation priced as three options. Send the hardware price and the three install prices separately.
  4. Two different dates. USAC lists December 4 as the earliest contract date. The RFP's proposal deadline is December 8, 12:00 noon CST. Work to December 8.

"Or equivalent" is allowed on every line. Alternatives must be clearly marked as different from the spec. Price each school separately with make, model or SKU, quantity, unit cost and extended cost.

How they will choose

No point scores are published. The RFP lists these, with price as the first criterion:

  • Price (the number one criterion)
  • Reliability and fast, proven service
  • Past work with Lake Zurich CUSD 95 (a plus)
  • School district experience, market share and references
  • Presence in the Chicago area

What gets a bid thrown out

  • Arriving after the due date
  • Not separating E-Rate ineligible items and their prices
  • Not addressing every requirement in the RFP and Form 470
  • Not offering the Lowest Corresponding Price
  • Responses that look like spam or are machine-generated

What you need to have and to send

  • Your SPIN on the proposal, active with USAC, not red-lighted
  • A service center and service staff in Illinois
  • Three school references the same size as the district or larger
  • Three E-Rate references in Illinois (preferred)
  • Certifications for all hardware and software you bid
  • Equipment bought from the manufacturer or an authorized distributor
  • Agreement to file your SPAC (Form 473) every year
  • Company overview: support staff, annual sales, school district work
  • Your standard sales contract and maintenance contract
  • Shipping, freight and other fees listed, if any
  • Insurance of at least $1,000,000 each for comprehensive, liability and workers' compensation, naming the district as additional insured (on award)
  • Prevailing wages for Lake County apply to any labor
  • Drug-free workforce and no bid-rigging certifications
