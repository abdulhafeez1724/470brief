Questions

Where does the data come from?

From public E-Rate records that USAC publishes, including each school's Form 470 and its RFP documents. We read them and write the summary. 470 Brief is independent and is not affiliated with USAC or the FCC.

Which states do you cover?

Any US state. Tell us yours when you ask for the free brief.

Can I rely on a brief instead of the RFP?

No. A brief tells you quickly whether a request is worth your time. Before you bid, read the RFP and check the USAC portal for any changes the school posted.

How fast do briefs arrive?

Within a week of a school filing its request, well inside the 28-day bidding window.
